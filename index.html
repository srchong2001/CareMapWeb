Why CareMap

The cost of waiting is measured in admissions—and margin

Plans sit on extraordinary signal in claims and clinical data, yet disengagement still shows up as ED visits and readmissions. CareMap, co-designed with Highmark, aligns analytics with how activation teams actually work: where to focus, who is ready, and what to do next.

The problem

Readmissions and downstream acute utilization drain budgets and member trust. Much of that spend is tied to members who were reachable earlier—but never fully activated.

The shift

CareMap combines claims and clinical signals with predictive models built for health-plan workflows, so leaders see where engagement is lagging and which interventions have the highest expected return.

Target ROI

Interventions that pay for themselves—many times over

Illustrative targets show how small, well-timed activation spend can offset catastrophic utilization. Figures are directional models for partner discussions, not guarantees.

40×
Medicare scenario

A $650 CareMap-class intervention mapped to roughly $27,750 in net savings by helping avoid a $28,400 complex readmission.

73×
Medicaid scenario

A $1,800 proactive outreach tied to roughly $131,700 in net savings by reducing the likelihood of a $133,500 Level-3 NICU stay.

At 50% Highmark engagement, modeled savings from stronger activation can reach on the order of $30 million annually—enough to fund innovation, stabilize margins, and protect member experience at the same time.
